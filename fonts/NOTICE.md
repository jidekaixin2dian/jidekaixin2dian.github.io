# Font notices

Both fonts are self-hosted and distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licenses are included beside the font files.

- `jk-text.woff2`: a character subset of Noto Sans SC from Google Fonts, with the weight axis limited to 400–800. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc . The derivative family is **JK Text**. Copyright and license notices are retained. See `Noto-OFL.txt`.
- `manrope-latin.woff2`: a Latin subset of Manrope from Google Fonts, with the weight axis limited to 400–800. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope . See `Manrope-OFL.txt`.

Character subsetting and family renaming are performed by `tools/subset-fonts.py`; glyph outlines are unchanged. Font files are part of this website, never served through a third-party CDN.
